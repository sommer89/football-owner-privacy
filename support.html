Football Owner — Support

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Need help? Email us at hello@sommerventures.dk and we’ll get back to you. There are no accounts to recover — your game saves live only on your device.

Contact us

For questions, bug reports, or feedback about Football Owner, email hello@sommerventures.dk. To help us respond quickly, it’s useful to include your device model, your iOS version, and the App version (shown in the game’s Settings).

Common questions

How are my saves stored? Your careers are saved only on your device. We run no servers and have no access to your saves. Deleting the App removes all local game data.

I lost my progress / changed devices. Because saves are local, they move only via your own Apple device or iCloud backup, under your Apple account. We can’t recover saves remotely.

Ads and in-app purchases. Rewarded video ads are optional and only run when you choose to watch one. In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. See our Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.

Purchase problems. For refunds or billing issues with an in-app purchase, contact Apple Support via reportaproblem.apple.com. For anything else, email us and we’ll help.
